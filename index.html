Services

Vertical UGC videos, made start to finish

Each project is a professionally produced vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts. You send the product, I handle the rest and deliver the finished files.

Included

  • Concept and creative direction
  • Script or talking pointsOr your own script, delivered as written
  • Filming and product demonstration
  • Editing and pacing
  • CaptionsBurned in, plus a caption file
  • The final exported video, ready to post
  • One round of revisions

Optional add-ons

  • Extra opening hooks
  • Alternative edits and cut-downs
  • Extra aspect ratiosFor example 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9
  • Raw footage
  • Extra revision rounds
  • Paid-ad usage licence
  • Longer usage periods

Usage. You can post the video organically on your brand’s own channels. Paid-ad use is licensed separately, for a set period.

What you’re buying. The video, not my audience. I don’t manage your accounts, post for you, run ads or promise posts on my own channels.

Pricing. Every brief is different, so I quote per project.

About

Hi, I’m Wisdom

I’m a software engineer and tech creator based in Ireland.

Working in software means I like to understand how a product actually works before I talk about it. My job on camera is to make that clear: a real demo, a fair test and plain English instead of a list of specs.

I plan, film and edit every video myself, with real products and no made-up claims. When a video shows a test, what you see and hear is the real result.

I also run ItsWizdom, my independent YouTube channel for real-world phone and gadget reviews. It’s separate from my paid UGC work.
